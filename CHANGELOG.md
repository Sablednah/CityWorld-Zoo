# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — unreleased

The first build. Minecraft 26.2 on NeoForge 26.2.0.72 or newer.

- Eight animals, each a spawn egg and breedable: giraffe, rhesus macaque, zebra, deer, lion, tiger,
  elephant, brown bear. Lions, tigers, bears and elephants fight back when hurt; the rest bolt.
- A CityWorld zoo datapack (CityWorld 6.3.0 or newer): eight animal entries over five habitats (savanna,
  woodland, scrubland, canopy, rockery). The giraffe's pen gets a taller fence; the elephant needs a big one.
- `#cityworld_zoo:zoo_animals`, an entity-type tag of all eight.
- `/zoo status` (what is registered, which build) and `/zoo count [radius]` (what is loaded near you).
- Placeholder textures and vanilla stand-in sounds, to be replaced.
