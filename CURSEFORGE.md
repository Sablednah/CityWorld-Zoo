# CityWorld: Zoo — the animals every zombie story walks past

**Eight zoo animals, each from a scene you remember.** The giraffes in the Salt Lake City overpass, and
the lab monkeys that never turned. The zebra in the snare. The deer in the snow and the herd on Fifth
Avenue. The lions that took it. Shiva. The elephant from Raccoon City's zoo. And a bear, because every
zoo has a bear.

They are ordinary Minecraft animals: a spawn egg each, they breed on their food, they wander, and the
ones with teeth fight back if you hurt them. No new mechanics to learn.

## With CityWorld

Install [CityWorld ReForged](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/cityworld-reforged) 6.3.0 or
newer beside it and the city's zoos fill with them: giraffes behind a taller fence, an elephant in a
big pen, lions on a savanna lot, macaques in the bamboo. No configuration. Without CityWorld, the
animals are still here, from the spawn eggs.

## Requirements

| Minecraft | NeoForge | Java |
|---|---|---|
| 26.2 | 26.2.0.72+ | 25 |

Install on the server and on every client. Commands for operators: `/zoo status`, `/zoo count`.
